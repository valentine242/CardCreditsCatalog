Privacy Policy

Card Credits · Effective November 13, 2026

Short version: Card Credits does not collect any data. The developer, Valentine Chan, receives nothing about you or how you use the app.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your cards and ticks. The cards you pick, who holds them, anniversary months, the credits you mark as used or hidden, and your reminder settings are stored on your device and in your own iCloud account, so your devices stay in step. The developer cannot see or reach this data.
  • Sharing with a partner. If you invite a partner, the shared list travels through Apple's iCloud sharing between your two iCloud accounts. You choose who to invite and can stop sharing at any time. The developer is not part of that exchange.
  • Reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

What the app downloads

About once a day the app downloads one public file, catalog.json, the list of cards and their benefits. The request contains nothing about you, your cards or your activity: everyone gets the same file. The file is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub receives the technical details of the request (such as an IP address) under its own privacy statement. The developer does not receive or have access to that information.

What the app never does

  • It never asks for a bank or card login, card numbers or account numbers.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • No third-party software kits (SDKs).
  • No account with the developer to create.

Links to card issuers

Each benefit links to the card issuer's own page. If you open one, that website's own privacy policy applies.

Support email

If you email support, your address and whatever you write are used only to answer you.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove the copy in your iCloud, open Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or Storage) → Card Credits → Delete Data. The app can also export your data to a file that you keep.

Children

Card Credits is for adults who hold credit cards and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
