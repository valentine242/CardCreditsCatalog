Common questions

Where do the benefit amounts and dates come from?

From the card issuers' own pages. The list is checked every week, and each benefit in the app shows a link to its issuer page and the date it was last checked.

A benefit looks wrong or is missing.

Open the benefit and tap "This looks wrong", or email the address above. Issuers change benefits often; your issuer's terms are always the final word.

Does the app connect to my bank or card account?

No. It never asks for a bank login. You tick off credits yourself as you use them.

How do I share the list with my partner?

Invite them from the Household screen. Sharing uses your own iCloud accounts, so both of you need to be signed in to iCloud.

Why does the app ask for my card's anniversary month?

Some credits reset on your card's anniversary instead of January 1. The app asks only for those cards, so it never has to guess a deadline.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for Card Credits in the Settings app, and that a reminder time is set in the app's Settings.

What does the app know about me?

Nothing leaves your device and your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.
