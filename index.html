Card Credits

An iPhone and iPad app by Valentine Chan

For households that pay annual fees on premium credit cards: pick the cards you hold, and Card Credits shows every credit grouped by category, what's left to use before each deadline, and reminds you in time. Partners share one list. It never asks for a bank login.

Where the benefit facts come from

The app reads one public file, catalog.json, published on this site. It is checked every week against the card issuers' own pages, and each benefit lists its source page and the date it was last checked.

Not financial advice. Always confirm terms with your card issuer.
